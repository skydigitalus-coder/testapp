About Pandora's Box

Local & Family-Owned Since Day One

Who We Are

Pandora's Box is local and family-owned, and we take great pride in serving our Washington and Oregon communities. As a family-owned business, we care deeply about our local community and create a welcoming environment for all our customers.

We are your ultimate destination for an unparalleled selection of pipes, kratom, detox, and vape products. Our staff is well-versed in the latest trends and innovations in the smoking and vaping industry, ready to help you find exactly what you need.

Whether you're looking for premium e-liquids, cutting-edge vaping devices from starter kits to advanced mods, quality glass pipes, or trusted kratom and detox products, we have something for everyone.

Our Commitment

We are dedicated to:

  • Offering quality products from trusted brands at fair prices
  • Providing excellent customer service and expert guidance
  • Creating a welcoming and comfortable environment for all customers
  • Supporting our local communities across Washington & Oregon
  • Staying current with the latest trends and innovations
