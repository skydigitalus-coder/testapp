Shipping & BOPIS Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

🚀 Buy Online, Pick Up In Store (BOPIS)

Our primary fulfillment method is BOPIS - shop online and pick up at any of our 3 convenient locations in Washington & Oregon!

1. Buy Online, Pick Up In Store (BOPIS)

How BOPIS Works

  1. Shop Online: Browse and add items to your cart on our website
  2. Select Location: Choose your preferred pickup location at checkout
  3. We Prepare: We'll gather your order and notify you when it's ready (typically 2-4 hours)
  4. Pick Up: Come to the store with your ID and order confirmation

BOPIS Locations

Pick up your online orders at any of our locations:

  • Kennewick, WA - 5300 W. Clearwater Avenue, Suite B
  • Richland, WA - 960 George Washington Way
  • Pendleton, OR - Contact for address

BOPIS Timeframes

  • Preparation Time: Most orders ready within 2-4 hours during business hours
  • Store Hours: Monday - Sunday, 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM (all locations)
  • Order Cutoff: Orders placed after 6:00 PM will be ready the next business day
  • Hold Period: We'll hold your order for 7 days; after that, it will be cancelled and refunded

What to Bring for Pickup

REQUIRED:

  • Valid government-issued photo ID proving you are 21+ (driver's license, passport, state ID)
  • Order confirmation (email or text message with order number)

Authorized Pickup: Someone other than the purchaser may pick up the order if they:

  • Are 21+ with valid ID
  • Have the order confirmation
  • Can verify the purchaser's name

2. Local Delivery (Select Areas)

We may offer local delivery in select areas around our store locations. Delivery options include:

  • Availability: Limited to areas within 15 miles of our stores
  • Minimum Order: $50 minimum for delivery
  • Delivery Fee: $10 flat rate (may vary by distance)
  • Timeframe: Same-day or next-day delivery based on availability
  • Age Verification: ID will be checked upon delivery (21+ required)

Contact your preferred store location to check if delivery is available in your area.

3. Shipping via Mail/Courier

Currently Not Available: Due to varying federal, state, and local regulations regarding the shipment of tobacco and vaping products, we do not currently offer shipping via USPS, UPS, FedEx, or other carriers.

We encourage customers to use our convenient BOPIS service instead!

4. Order Processing

  • Order Confirmation: You'll receive an email/text when your order is placed
  • Ready Notification: You'll receive another notification when your order is ready for pickup
  • Changes: Contact the store immediately if you need to modify or cancel your order
  • Questions: Call (509) 396-9700 or email pandorasbox_123@yahoo.com

5. Pricing & Fees

  • BOPIS: FREE! No pickup fees
  • Local Delivery: $10 flat rate (where available)
  • Taxes: Applicable state and local taxes will be added at checkout
  • Payment: Full payment required at time of order

6. Product Availability

Product availability may vary by location. If an item is out of stock at your selected location:

  • We'll contact you with alternative options
  • You may choose a different pickup location
  • You may modify or cancel your order for a full refund

7. Age-Restricted Products

All orders containing age-restricted products require:

  • Age verification (21+) at time of purchase
  • Valid ID presentation at pickup/delivery
  • Signature confirmation

We will NOT release orders to anyone who:

  • Cannot provide valid ID proving they are 21+
  • Appears intoxicated or impaired
  • We reasonably believe will provide products to minors

8. Cancellations & Modifications

  • Before Preparation: Orders can be cancelled for a full refund
  • After Preparation: Contact the store; cancellations subject to standard return policy
  • Unclaimed Orders: Orders not picked up within 7 days will be automatically cancelled and refunded
  • Refund Timeline: Refunds processed within 5-7 business days
